## 🌟 Project Overview
The **PR. 2 Analyzer** is an advanced Excel-based business analytics model that evaluates regional sales performance, product profitability, customer purchasing behavior, and predictive financial trends. By combining the Data Analysis Toolpak, statistical modeling, What-If scenario planning, and an interactive dashboard, it turns raw sales records into actionable business intelligence.

---

## 🎯 Business Questions Answered
* Which regions and product categories generate the most revenue?
* How strongly do sales drive profit?
* How do different discount rates affect net profit margins?
* Who are the top customers by total purchase volume?
* How are sales trending over time, and is growth positive or negative?

---

## 🛠️ Key Topics & Technical Skills Covered
* **Advanced Statistical Analysis:** Descriptive statistics (mean, median, mode, standard deviation, variance, kurtosis, skewness).
* **Predictive Modeling:** Linear regression (Sales vs. Profit) using the Data Analysis Toolpak.
* **Scenario Planning:** What-If Analysis of how variable discount rates affect net profit margins.
* **Lookup & Reference Functions:** `INDEX()` and `MATCH()` combinations alongside ranking formulas.
* **Formatting & Automation:** Automated timestamps (`NOW()`) and custom number formats with conditional symbols/arrows for sales growth trends.
* **Conditional Formatting:** Automatic highlighting of top-performing customers by total purchase volume.
* **Data Aggregation & Storytelling:** Multi-regional pivot tables and an executive dashboard.

## 📈 Key Analytical Highlights
* **Total Sales Volume:** Regional sales surpassing **$195,217** across **200 transactions**.
* **Regression Performance:** Multiple R of **0.7734** and R-Square of **0.5981**, showing a moderate-to-strong positive relationship between sales and profit. Roughly 60% of the variation in profit is explained by sales.
* **Top Regions:** West and Central led in revenue.
* **Top Categories:** Electronics and Clothing were the highest-demand categories.

---

## 💡 Business Insights & Recommendations
1. **Double down on West and Central:** Focus inventory and marketing where revenue is strongest.
2. **Protect margins on discounts:** Since sales explain only about 60% of profit, discounting and costs clearly matter. Use the What-If sheet to find the discount level where profit starts to erode.
3. **Reward top customers:** Use the highlighted high-volume customers for loyalty programs or targeted offers.
4. **Review underperformers:** Investigate low-revenue regions and categories for pricing or distribution issues.
